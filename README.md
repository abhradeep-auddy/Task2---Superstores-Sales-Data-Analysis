Product Sales & Profit Analysis Dashboard (Advanced) - Task 2

---> Project Overview
This project involves an advanced analysis of a retail dataset to evaluate multi-dimensional performance across regions, product categories, and customer segments. By integrating complex data sources, the project identifies critical revenue drivers and explores the relationship between high-volume sales and standardized profit margins to support 2025 business planning.  

---> Tools Used
- Excel → Advanced data cleaning, pivot table generation, and performance metrics calculation.
- SQL (PostgreSQL) → Data transformation and structural joins to prepare the dataset for visualization.
- Power BI → Development of an interactive dashboard featuring KPI cards, trend lines, and regional sales distribution.

---> Key Insights
- Regional Performance: The North region leads the market with $1.15 Million in total sales, followed by the East and Central regions.
- Standardized Profitability: All major product categories (Laptops, Tablets, Monitors, etc.) maintain a highly consistent 30.0% profit margin.
- Customer Segmentation: The Consumer segment remains the primary driver of the business, accounting for 57.19% ($2.81M) of the total sales.
- Discount Impact: While discounting does not lead to net losses, it reduces the average profit per order from $923.85 to $851.59.
- Q1 Sales Peak: The business shows strong early-year momentum, with sales peaking in March ($609K) before experiencing a gradual decline toward the end of the year.

---> Business Recommendations
- North Region Expansion: Continue to prioritize inventory and marketing spend in the North region as it currently shows the highest customer engagement.
- Mid-Year Sales Strategy: Implement targeted promotions in the second half of the year (specifically July–December) to counteract the observed seasonal decline.
- Protect Profit Margins: Limit the use of discounts on high-performing products to maintain the $900+ average profit per transaction.
- Segment Loyalty: Focus on retention strategies for the Consumer segment, which contributes more than half of the total revenue.
- Inventory Optimization: Use the Q1 peak data to plan stock levels for the high-demand months of January and March.
